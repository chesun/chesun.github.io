## thru-hike, august 2026

From August 1 to 13, 2026, I thru-hiked the Tahoe Rim Trail, a ~170-mile loop around Lake Tahoe. The trail circles the lake through ancient junipers, lupine meadows, volcanic ridgelines, and — my favorite — the granite lake basins of Desolation Wilderness.
