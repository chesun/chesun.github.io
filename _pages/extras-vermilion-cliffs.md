## buckskin gulch and paria canyon, march 2026

In March 2026 I backpacked through Buckskin Gulch — one of the longest slot canyons in the world — and down the Paria Canyon to Lees Ferry. Days of walking between glowing sandstone walls, wading the muddy river as the canyon slowly opened into desert.
